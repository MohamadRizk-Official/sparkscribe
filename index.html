    How it works

    1. 1Upload a lectureAudio or video, even hours long. It's transcribed on your device, with speakers and timestamps.
    2. 2Get it organizedSave it to your library, or a course, and get a summary and organized notes.
    3. 3Study itFlashcards, practice quizzes and a study guide. Ask questions and jump to the moment it was said.
    4. 4Come back to reviewPick up where you left off. Questions you missed are kept, so you can practise them again.
