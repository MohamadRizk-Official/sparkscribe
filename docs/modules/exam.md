# Exam prep (`src/home/exam.js`)

A course's exam date and topics become a countdown and a plan built **only from the student's own records in that course**. Nothing is generated by AI and nothing is estimated: no readiness scores, no invented weak spots. Covered by `tests/exam-plan-test.mjs` (the pure functions; the card and dialog need a browser).

## Pure helpers
- `daysUntil(iso, now)`: whole calendar days from today (local date, time of day ignored) to a `YYYY-MM-DD` date. Negative once the exam has passed.
- `inDays(n)`: `today`, `tomorrow`, `in N days`.
- `fmtExamDate(iso)`: a short weekday + month + day label in the browser's locale.
- `parseTopics(text)`: splits on commas, semicolons and new lines; trims; drops anything shorter than 2 characters and repeats (case-insensitive, the first spelling is kept); each topic is cut to 80 characters; at most 10 topics.
- `pace(steps, days)`: steps per day, `ceil(steps / (days - 1))`, so the last day before the exam stays free for a final look-over. `null` when there are no steps or fewer than 2 days left.

## `buildPlan(lectures)`
Each lecture is `{ id, title, kinds:Set, missed, hasQuiz, quizTaken, due }`. The steps come out in this order, each tied to its lecture:

| order | when | step | opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | none of `flashcards`, `quiz`, `study_guide`, `exam_points`, `definitions` exists (notes and summaries don't count) | Make flashcards or a practice quiz | – |
| 2 | `missed > 0` | Retake N missed question(s) | quiz |
| 3 | `due > 0` | Review N flashcard(s) | flashcards |
| 4 | a quiz exists and has not been taken | Take the practice quiz | quiz |

One lecture can appear in several groups. A lecture with study material and nothing outstanding gives no step.

## Where the data comes from
`loadCourse` reads the course's lectures (`courseLectureIds`), their ready insights and artifacts from Supabase: quiz progress gives `missed` and `quizTaken`, the flashcard set's spaced-review schedule (`readSrs`, `dueIndexes` from `src/insights/srs.js`) gives `due`. For each topic the student listed, `searchLibrary` finds the lectures in the course that mention it.
